Dark Divine didn’t start in a boardroom. It started with a serpent, a number, and the idea that what you wear should say something before you do.

Every piece runs through the same hands: designed in-house, sampled until the fit is right, and produced in one small run. No warehouse of leftovers. No re-releases. The 00 on the chest isn’t a size — it’s a marker that you were there for the run.

We’d rather sell out than mass-produce. That’s the whole model.

Questions

Before You Ask

How does sizing run?
Jerseys and tees run true to size with a relaxed athletic cut — size up if you want it oversized. Nylon pants are a relaxed straight leg; size down for a tapered stack. Every product page has fit notes and model sizing, and the full size guide has flat measurements.
When will my order ship?
Orders ship within 2 business days from the US. Standard delivery is 3–7 business days, and you'll get a tracking number by email the moment it leaves. Full details in the shipping policy.
What's the return policy?
30 days, unworn with tags, full refund to your original payment — no restocking fee, no interrogation. Drop items are final sale only when marked on the product page. Details in returns & refunds.
Will sold-out pieces restock?
No. One production run per colorway is the rule the brand is built on. If a size sells out before the run does, join the back-in-stock list on the product page — remaining units sometimes free up from unpaid orders.
Is checkout secure?
Yes — payments run through Stripe/Shopify with 256-bit SSL encryption. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. We never see or store your card number.
How do I get a drop access code?
Join the email list below. Access codes go out to the list before every drop — that's the only place they're published.
