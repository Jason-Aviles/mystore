Policy

Shipping

Processing

Orders ship from the US within 2 business days. During a drop weekend, allow up to 4 business days — every piece is checked and packed by hand.

Rates & Delivery

  • US Standard — $6.95, free over $100 · 3–7 business days
  • US Priority — $14.95 · 2–3 business days
  • Canada — $14.95 · 7–14 business days
  • International — calculated at checkout · 10–21 business days

International orders may be subject to customs duties set by your country — those are collected by your carrier, not by us.

Tracking

You'll get a tracking number by email the moment your order leaves. Nothing after 3 business days? Resend it here or email support@darkdivine.store.

Lost or Stolen Packages

If tracking shows delivered but nothing arrived, contact us within 7 days — we'll open a carrier claim and make it right. You will not be left hanging.
